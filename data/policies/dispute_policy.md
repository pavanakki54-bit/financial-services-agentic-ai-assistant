# Transaction Dispute Policy

## Purpose

This synthetic policy describes the transaction dispute process used by the Financial Services Agentic AI Assistant portfolio project.

## Eligibility

Customers may dispute eligible posted card transactions when they believe a transaction is unauthorized, duplicated, incorrectly charged, or when goods or services were not received as expected.

Pending transactions are not eligible for dispute review until they become posted transactions.

## Reporting Window

Customers should report eligible transaction disputes within 60 days of the transaction appearing on their statement.

## Required Information

A dispute request should include:

- Transaction ID
- Transaction date
- Transaction amount
- Merchant name
- Reason for the dispute
- Any available supporting information

## Unauthorized Transactions

Customers who identify an unauthorized transaction should report it promptly.

Additional verification may be required before the dispute is processed.

## Review

Submitting a dispute does not guarantee approval.

Each dispute is reviewed according to the applicable policy and available transaction information.

## Disclaimer

This document contains fictional policy information created only for an independent AI engineering portfolio project. It does not represent the policy of any real financial institution.
